Backup and reset
"Download settings" saves a file with your alliances, kingdoms, times, reminders and events, without your day-by-day notes and battles: good for giving to someone. "Download full backup" keeps everything. "Load file" reads either one (it replaces all your data; you are asked to confirm).
"Show this alliance" puts the alliance chosen at the top and its kingdom here as text, to give to someone. "Show everything" puts all your data here, to move it to another browser or device. Paste either kind and press Import: an alliance is added; "everything" replaces all your data (you are asked to tap twice).
Empty boxes in event icons are Kingshot's own in-game icons: they look blank here but show correctly when pasted in the game chat. Dates use the game's UTC day. Edit events.json (for whoever publishes the game events).
